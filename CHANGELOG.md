# Changelog

## Unreleased

`<SiteWorld doorStyle>`: `"steel"` (the default, as before) or `"plain"`, a flat unlit slab with no frame, no
textures and no door lighting. New exports: `DoorStyle`, `DEFAULT_DOOR_STYLE`, `doorsNeedLighting`, `doorParts`,

First public release, extracted from a private operations dashboard: `<SiteWorld>`, the room contract,
terminals, doors, walk mode, `GridFloor`/`WallBoxes`, and the optional demon game.
